# BLK ANVL — Image production prompts

Generated from `src/data/catalog.ts` by `scripts/image-prompts.mjs`. Use these to brief a photographer or to generate imagery with an image model that can take the logo as a reference.

Brand mark reference: /public/brand/lockup-light.svg — monogram is two sharp angular blades forming an "A" (tall blade on the right over a Λ with a hooked left foot); wordmark "BLK ANVL" in wide, widely spaced geometric capitals where A and V have no crossbars. Branding is always tonal, small and understated — never oversized.

**Global style:** Premium athletic apparel e-commerce photography, consistent across the catalogue, true-to-life fabric texture and construction, accurate stitching, no distorted anatomy, no extra fingers, logos legible and physically following folds, perspective and lighting, no other brand logos.

**Studio shots:** seamless warm light-grey background (#E6E4DF), soft overhead key light from upper left, gentle floor shadow, ghost-mannequin or flat-pressed presentation, 4:5 crop, garment filling ~80% of frame height.

**Lifestyle shots:** monochrome-leaning colour grade, dark industrial training space or minimal concrete studio, hard directional light, editorial poses, 4:5 crop.

Drop finished files into `public/products/<slug>/<colour>-<shot>.webp` (shots: front, back, detail, texture) — the site picks them up automatically. Add lifestyle images to the product's image list in `src/lib/products.ts`.
